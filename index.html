General Layout Guidelines

1-inch margins on all four sides, Times New Roman, 12-point font for main text, 10-point font for footnotes. Double-spaced body text, single-spaced footnotes and block quotes. Top-right or bottom-center page numbers (omit page number on title page).

Reference Rules & Manual Examples

Standard Book (Single Author)
First Note (N): 1. Firstname Lastname, Title of Book: Subtitle (City: Publisher, Year), Page.
Short Note (SN): 2. Lastname, Short Title, Page.
Bibliography (B): Lastname, Firstname. Title of Book: Subtitle. City: Publisher, Year.
Key differences: First footnotes use commas and enclose publication details in parentheses. Bibliography items use trailing periods and hanging indents.
Journal Article
First Note (N): 1. Author Firstname Lastname, "Title of the Article," Journal Title Volume Number, no. Issue (Year): Page.
Short Note (SN): 2. Lastname, "Short Article Title," Page.
Bibliography (B): Lastname, Author Firstname. "Title of the Article." Journal Title Volume Number, no. Issue (Year): Full Page Range.
Commentary Volume (In a Series)
First Note (N): 1. Scholar Firstname Lastname, The Book of Genesis, Series Title 1A (City: Publisher, Year), Page.
Bibliography (B): Lastname, Scholar Firstname. The Book of Genesis. Series Title 1A. City: Publisher, Year.
Warning: Commentary series formats vary. Verify whether your series treats volumes as authored or edited compilations.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Review this diagnostic checklist before uploading or submitting your final paper draft.

1. The Extinction of "Ibid."

SBL 2nd Edition completely banned the use of "Ibid." for consecutive duplicate citations.

Incorrect Form

1. Smith, Genesis, 12.
2. Ibid., 14.

Correct SBL 2nd Edition Form

1. Smith, Genesis, 12.
2. Smith, Genesis, 14.

2. The Bible Bibliography Trap

Modern translation editions of Holy Scripture are cited textually in inline parenthetical blocks. Do not list standard Bible translations in your trailing Bibliography index!

Incorrect Form

Adding "The New Revised Standard Version. Nashville: Nelson, 1989" into your structural bibliography page.

Correct SBL 2nd Edition Form

Incorporate the scriptural reference directly in your body prose: "...as stated in the text (Gen 1:1 NRSV)."

3. Punctuation Placement with Footnotes

Footnote callout numbers must sit outside trailing punctuation marks like periods or quotation marks.

Incorrect Form

According to research3, the context changes. Or "This is true."4

Correct SBL 2nd Edition Form

According to research, the context changes.3 Or "This is true."4
