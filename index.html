Reference Rules & Manual Examples

Standard Book (Single Author)
First Note (N): 1. Firstname Lastname, Title of Book: Subtitle (City: Publisher, Year), Page.
Short Note (SN): 2. Lastname, Short Title, Page.
Bibliography (B): Lastname, Firstname. Title of Book: Subtitle. City: Publisher, Year.
Key differences: First footnotes use commas and enclose publication details in parentheses. Bibliography items use trailing periods and hanging indents.
Journal Article
First Note (N): 1. Firstname Lastname, "Title of the Article," Journal Title Volume Number, no. Issue (Year): Page.
Short Note (SN): 2. Lastname, "Short Article Title," Page.
Bibliography (B): Lastname, Firstname. "Title of the Article." Journal Title Volume Number, no. Issue (Year): Full Page Range.
Commentary Volume (In a Series)
First Note (N): 1. Firstname Lastname, The Book of Genesis, Series Title 1A (City: Publisher, Year), Page.
Bibliography (B): Lastname, Firstname. The Book of Genesis. Series Title 1A. City: Publisher, Year.
Warning: Commentary series formats vary. Verify whether your series treats volumes as authored or edited compilations.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Review this diagnostic checklist before uploading or submitting your final paper draft.

1. The Extinction of "Ibid."

SBL 2nd Edition completely banned the use of "Ibid." for consecutive duplicate citations.

Incorrect Form

1. Smith, Genesis, 12.
2. Ibid., 14.

Correct SBL 2nd Edition Form

1. Smith, Genesis, 12.
2. Smith, Genesis, 14.

2. The Bibliography Triple 3-Em Dash (——.)

When citing multiple works by the exact same author in your Bibliography, replace the author's name in subsequent entries with a 3-em dash (3 consecutive em dashes or 6 hyphens) followed by a period.

Incorrect Form

Wright, N. T. Climax of the Covenant...
Wright, N. T. Jesus and the Victory of God...

Correct SBL 2nd Edition Form

Wright, N. T. Climax of the Covenant...
———. Jesus and the Victory of God...

3. The Bible Bibliography Trap & Verse Citation Format

Modern translation editions of Holy Scripture are cited textually in inline parenthetical blocks. Do not list standard Bible translations in your trailing Bibliography index! Furthermore, use a colon (not a period) to separate chapter and verse.

Incorrect Form

Adding "The New Revised Standard Version. Nashville: Nelson, 1989" into your bibliography, or citing verse as Rom 1.16.

Correct SBL 2nd Edition Form

Incorporate the reference directly in prose using a colon: "...as stated in the text (Rom 1:16 NRSV)."

4. Punctuation Placement with Footnotes

Footnote callout numbers must sit outside trailing punctuation marks like periods or quotation marks.

Incorrect Form

According to research3, the context changes. Or "This is true."4

Correct SBL 2nd Edition Form

According to research, the context changes.3 Or "This is true."4

5. Possessives for Proper Names Ending in "S"

SBL style requires adding an apostrophe and an 's' for proper names ending in 's' or an 's' sound (e.g., Jesus's, Moses's), rather than just an apostrophe.

Incorrect Form

Jesus' disciples or Moses' law.

Correct SBL 2nd Edition Form

Jesus's disciples or Moses's law.

6. Parenthetical Scripture References & Biblical Abbreviations

Primary biblical references belong in parenthetical citations in the main body text (e.g., "...as Paul notes [Rom 1:16]"), rather than in footnotes, unless they are part of a broader discursive note. Additionally, do not include periods in biblical book abbreviations.

Incorrect Form

Gen. 1:1, Matt. 5:3, or 1 Cor. 13:4.

Correct SBL 2nd Edition Form

Gen 1:1, Matt 5:3, or 1 Cor 13:4.

7. Era Designations (BCE / CE)

Use BCE and CE in all capital letters with no internal periods (do not use B.C.E., C.E., or BC/AD).

Incorrect Form

50 B.C.E. or 70 A.D.

Correct SBL 2nd Edition Form

50 BCE or 70 CE

8. Numbers, Specialized Terminology, and Transliterations

Follow standard SBL conventions regarding numerals, technical terminology capitalization, and non-Latin character transliteration.

Incorrect Form

• 84 manuscripts were examined.
• Reading the gospel of Matthew.
• Inserting raw un-transliterated Hebrew without proper fonts or rendering.

Correct SBL 2nd Edition Form

• Spell out numbers lower than 100 in prose: "eighty-four manuscripts."
• Lowercase general "gospel," but capitalize specific canonical books: "Gospel of Matthew."
• Use standard SBL Latin character transliterations for Hebrew, Greek, and Arabic when specialized fonts are absent.

These general issues regarding punctuation, numbers, terminology, and transliteration are discussed at length in §4 and §5 (pages 12–67) of SBLHS.

9. Structural & Institutional Formatting Rules

Verify structural notes regarding footnotes, pagination, and sorting conventions before final submission.

Incorrect Form

• Using Endnotes instead of Footnotes.
• Placing footnote text on a different page than its callout.
• Sorting Bibliography entries by book title or publication order.
• Missing spelling or capitalization errors in source author names.

Correct SBL 2nd Edition Form

• Footnotes only: North Central utilizes bottom-of-page footnotes (endnotes are not permitted).
• Footnote text must reside at the bottom of the same page as its number callout.
• Sort Bibliography entries in alphabetical order by the author's last name.
• Double-check spelling and pagination using Page Breaks for clear section splits.
