Document Formatting Matrix

Set these standards up in Microsoft Word or Google Docs before writing your paper:

Element SBL Rule
Margins 1 inch on all four sides.
Font Times New Roman, 12pt for main text. 10pt for footnotes.
Line Spacing Double-spaced for body text. Single-spaced inside individual footnotes and block quotes.
Page Numbers Top-right corner. Suppressed (hidden) on the Title Page.

Citation Formula Templates

SBL requires a First Footnote (N) format, a shortened Subsequent Footnote (SN) format, and a Bibliography (B) format.

1. Standard Book (Single Author)

First Footnote (N)
1. Firstname Lastname, Title of Book: Subtitle (City: Publisher, Year), Page Number.
Subsequent Footnote (SN)
2. Lastname, Short Title, Page Number.
Bibliography (B)
Lastname, Firstname. Title of Book: Subtitle. City: Publisher, Year.

2. Commentary in a Series

First Footnote (N)
1. Firstname Lastname, Book Title, Series Title Vol Number (City: Publisher, Year), Page.
Real Example
1. John J. Collins, Daniel, Hermeneia (Minneapolis: Fortress, 1993), 45.

SBL "Before You Submit" Diagnostic Checklist

Avoid these common, high-frequency deduction errors before finalizing your graduate assignments:

The Ibid. Trap

Do not use "Ibid." when citing the same source consecutively. SBL 2nd Edition completely eliminated its usage.

✓ The SBL Solution

Always use the author's last name, a shortened title, and the page number for all subsequent citations.

The Bible Bibliography Error

Do not include standard modern Bible translations (e.g., NRSV, ESV, NIV) inside your final bibliography.

✓ The SBL Solution

Cite Bible translations parenthetically inside your main text text only (e.g., Gen 1:1 NRSV), never in the back bibliography.

Footnote Punctuation Splitting

Do not separate authors and book names with periods in footnotes like you do in a bibliography.

✓ The SBL Solution

Footnotes flow like sentences. Use commas between names and titles, and wrap your publication info cleanly in parentheses.
