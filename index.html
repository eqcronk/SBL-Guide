Format My Paper

Follow this structure when creating your document layout in Microsoft Word or Google Docs.

The Layout

1 inch margins on all four sides, Times New Roman, 12-point scaling, Times New Roman, 10-point scaling for footnotes, Double-spaced for main text body. Single-spaced inside individual footnotes and long block quotes, Top right corner or bottom center page numbers, Do not show numbers on the introductory title page.

Cite a Source

SBL uses footnotes at the bottom of each page, alongside a comprehensive Bibliography list at the very end of your paper.

Standard Book (Single Author)
First Note (N): 1. Firstname Lastname, Title of Book: Subtitle (City: Publisher, Year), Page.
Short Note (SN): 2. Lastname, Short Title, Page.
Bibliography (B): Lastname, Firstname. Title of Book: Subtitle. City: Publisher, Year.
Key differences: First footnotes use commas and separate publication parameters with parentheses. Bibliography items use trailing periods and a hanging indent.
Journal Article
First Note (N): 1. Author Firstname Lastname, "Title of the Article," Journal Title Volume Number, no. Issue (Year): Page.
Short Note (SN): 2. Lastname, "Short Article Title," Page.
Bibliography (B): Lastname, Author Firstname. "Title of the Article." Journal Title Volume Number, no. Issue (Year): Full Page Range.
Commentary Volume (In a Series)
First Note (N): 1. Scholar Firstname Lastname, The Book of Genesis, Series Title 1A (City: Publisher, Year), Page.
Bibliography (B): Lastname, Scholar Firstname. The Book of Genesis. Series Title 1A. City: Publisher, Year.
Warning: Commentary series are diverse. Always cross-check if your series is treated like an authored volume or an edited compilation entry.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Review this diagnostic checklist before uploading or passing in your final paper draft to safeguard your grades.

1. The Extinction of "Ibid."

SBL 2nd Edition completely banned the use of "Ibid." to denote consecutive duplicate citation resources.

Incorrect Form

1. Smith, Genesis, 12.
2. Ibid., 14.

Correct SBL 2nd Edition Form

1. Smith, Genesis, 12.
2. Smith, Genesis, 14.

2. The Bible Bibliography Trap

Modern translation editions of Holy Scripture are only cited textually inside inline parenthetical blocks. Never list them in your trailing Bibliography index!

Incorrect Form

Adding "The New Revised Standard Version. Nashville: Nelson, 1989" into your structural bibliography pages.

Correct SBL 2nd Edition Form

Incorporate the scriptural span directly into your main text prose body: "...as stated in the absolute narrative baseline text (Gen 1:1 NRSV)."

3. Punctuation Placement with Footnotes

Footnote lookup callout index numerals must sit outside trailing punctuation marks like periods or quotes.

Incorrect Form

According to research3, the context changes. Or alternative layout traps like "This is true."4

Correct SBL 2nd Edition Form

According to comprehensive research, the historical framework context changes dynamically.3
